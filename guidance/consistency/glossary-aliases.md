Repeated slugs (for example `embedding`, `checkpoint`, and `recall-at-k`) are covered by `scout-glossary.md`; they are not repeated here. “Caller” means a lesson glossary link, followed by the implementation site when it is useful for the relink.

## Preserve these as distinct concepts

- **`parity-check` (ch47) vs `framework-parity` (ch55):** keep both, but make the relationship explicit. `parity-check` is the general numerical comparison under tolerances (caller [`chapters/47/lesson.html:49`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/47/lesson.html:49)); `framework-parity` is the cross-runtime instance that also fixes layout, dtype, loss reduction, update, and serialization (caller [`chapters/55/lesson.html:6`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/55/lesson.html:6)). Define the latter as “A parity check across execution frameworks under a shared numerical contract,” or link ch55 to the general term and retain the specialized slug only if its extra contract is taught.
- **`identity-shortcut` (ch21) vs `residual-connection` (ch21):** the former is the parameter-free route; the latter is the route plus learned branch and sum. Do not merge.
- **`feature-scaling` (ch03) vs `standardization` (ch14):** scaling is the umbrella; standardization is the mean/subtract and standard-deviation/divide subtype. Callers [`chapters/03/lesson.html`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/03/lesson.html), [`chapters/14/lesson.html`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/14/lesson.html).
- **`normalization` (ch21), `layer-normalization` (ch36), `embedding-normalization` (ch52), and `standardization` (ch14):** these differ by object and axes. LayerNorm normalizes features within each token row; embedding normalization rescales one representation to unit length; feature standardization uses training-set statistics. Keep the family cross-linked, with axes/object named in every definition.
- **`representation` (ch22) vs `embedding` (ch24/ch33):** an embedding is a learned vector representation, but representations can be non-embedding features or coordinates. Keep `embedding` broad enough to include encoder-computed vectors such as ch52, not only ID-table lookup rows. Callers [`chapters/22/lesson.html`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/22/lesson.html), [`chapters/24/lesson.html`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/24/lesson.html), [`chapters/33/lesson.html`](/home/bigsmarty/Projects/ml/chapters/33/lesson.html).
- **`recall` (ch05) vs `recall-at-k` (ch24/ch52):** classification recall is `TP/(TP+FN)`; retrieval Recall@k asks whether relevant items occur in the top `k` and uses a per-query relevance denominator. Preserve the slugs and keep the task type in each definition.
- **`checkpoint` (ch11/ch38), `checkpoint-recovery` (ch50), `model-artifact` (ch53), and `training-identity` (ch56):** snapshot, restore action, deployable package, and continuation metadata are different objects/operations. Do not merge.
- **`backpropagation` (ch07) vs `bptt` (ch23):** BPTT is backpropagation through an unrolled recurrent computation, with time-indexed state and shared parameters. Keep the specialization.
- **`softmax` (ch10) vs `online-softmax` (ch42):** the former is the normalization function; the latter is a streaming/stable algorithm for computing it without materializing all values. Keep both.
- **`top-k-routing` (ch48) vs `top-k-sampling` (ch40):** routing selects expert branches; sampling selects output tokens. The shared `top-k` phrase does not make the operations aliases.

## Consistency rule for future additions

Use a new slug only when the concept changes by mathematical object, axis/denominator, or operational contract. A numerical method, stable parameterization, or framework-specific caller should normally be prose under the canonical term. When a specialized term is retained, define its relationship to the broader term in one sentence and link the broader glossary entry from the lesson.
