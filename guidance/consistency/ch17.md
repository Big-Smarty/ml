## Shapes, storage, and reductions

- `Vec2 = [f64; 2]` stores one feature row, mean, or component; `Mat2 = [[f64; 2]; 2]` stores the row-major covariance matrix.
- `fit`: rows `[n, 2]` to fitted `Pca1`; sample covariance divides by `n - 1`.
- `transform`: raw features `[2]` to one scalar projection score after subtracting the fitted mean.
- `reconstruct`: one projection score to approximate features `[2]`.
- `mean_squared_reconstruction_norm`: sums the two squared coordinate residuals within each row, then divides by `n` rows. It deliberately differs from coordinate MSE, whose denominator would be `2n` here.
- `explained_fraction`: leading eigenvalue divided by covariance trace, with zero retained fraction by convention for constant data.
