## Shapes, reductions, and distinct quantities

- `Point = [f64; 2]`; fit inputs have logical shape `[n, 2]`, k-means centers `[k, 2]`, assignments `[n]`, and mixture responsibilities `[k]` for one point.
- `squared_distance` sums two squared coordinate differences. It has no square root and no average.
- `KMeans.inertia` remains the standard sum of the `n` final assigned squared distances.
- `GaussianMixture::log_density` is one point's scalar log density. `log_likelihood_sum` sums those values over `n` points; it does not average them and is not presented as comparable to a mean loss or to inertia.
- Component log terms are unnormalized `log(component.weight) + log_gaussian(features, component)` values. Max-shifted exponentials are relative masses. Dividing each by their total returns responsibilities, which are normalized per-point probabilities summing to one. Fitted mixture weights remain a separate normalized vector over components.
- `anomaly_score` remains negative log density for one point. It is a ranking score, not a class prediction or a calibrated alarm.
