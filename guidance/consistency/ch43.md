## Loss, targets, shapes, and learned state

- SFT uses explicit input IDs [T] and shifted hard next-token targets [T]. `loss_and_gradient` returns mean cross-entropy over T positions and derivatives for all 784 decoder parameters; direct SGD updates all of them.
- `average_loss` averages the two equal-length sequence means, which is also the mean over their six target positions.
- LoRA preserves `output_weight` [D,V] and uses A[D,r] times B[r,V] to form a [D,V] update. The base decoder remains frozen; only the 32 A/B values update. Factor derivatives are computed from the same old factor state.
- Distillation keeps the SFT teacher frozen and updates every student parameter. A hard target is one vocabulary ID; the soft target is the teacher probability vector [V]. For one token position (T=1) at temperature tau=1, the weighted sum of V hard-target losses and derivatives equals soft cross-entropy and its derivatives.
- Evaluation reports mean KL over the transfer tokens. Temperature is written tau in prose so T remains the sequence-length symbol. The executable intentionally implements only tau=1; the lesson explains the derivative scaling and common tau-squared loss scaling for tau greater than one.
