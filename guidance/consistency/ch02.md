## Loss, data, and numerical conventions

- Every tuple is `(input, target)`. `TRAIN` has shape `[5]` of scalar pairs; prediction maps one scalar to one scalar.
- `loss` is ordinary mean squared error over all N examples, with no half factor.
- Both gradient implementations differentiate that mean reduction. Central differences use `H = 1e-5`; comparisons use `1e-6 + 1e-4 * max(abs(a), abs(b))`.
- `step` computes both derivatives at the unchanged neuron before updating either parameter.
- `loss`, `gradient`, `numerical_gradient`, `step`, and `train` reject invalid or nonfinite results. `train` validates data, model state, and learning rate before its loop, including when `steps == 0`.
