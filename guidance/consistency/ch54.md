## Shapes, reductions, and preserved behavior

- One model input is `[f64; 2]`; one audit dataset is `&[Row]`; `logit`, `probability`, `predict`, and per-example `loss` perform no dataset reduction.
- `train_on_members` filters rows with `member == true`, retains 400 updates at learning rate 0.05, and divides both weight gradients and the bias gradient by the same number of member rows.
- `group_metrics` divides accuracy and positive prediction rate by group count, TPR by positive labels, and FPR by negative labels. `rate(numerator, denominator)` returns `None` for an empty denominator.
- Robustness still tests both `-radius` and `+radius` on feature zero for all 12 rows, sums endpoint decision flips, and takes the maximum absolute probability change.
- Membership attack TPR and FPR retain member and nonmember denominators. Association contrasts retain pooled-count crude reduction and the unweighted mean of stratum-specific differences.
- The 12-row fixture, model parameters, threshold, member-only training, membership threshold, parser schema/version and bounds, CLI text, error behavior, and numerical tolerances are unchanged.
