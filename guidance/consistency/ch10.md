## Classifier, shape, and reduction conventions

- Chapter 4 binary classification: one logit, one positive-class probability, and a class selected at a threshold.
- Chapter 10 multiclass classification: ten logits, ten softmax probabilities, and the greatest-logit class index. Softmax preserves the same ordering.
- One parsed image is `[in_features]`; model weights are `[10, in_features]`; bias and logits are `[10]`. This is Chapter 9's dense `[out_features, in_features]` layout with `out_features = 10`.
- `cross_entropy_from_logits` returns one example's loss. Training first accumulates weight and bias gradient sums, then scales them by `learning_rate / minibatch_len`, giving the gradient of mean loss over that minibatch. `metrics` averages loss and accuracy over all supplied examples.
- The stable maximum-shifted softmax and log-sum-exp arithmetic is unchanged. IDX byte layout, parser checks, generated fixtures, CLI modes, and numerical settings are unchanged.
