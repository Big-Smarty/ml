## Numerical and training contracts

- `Model::loss(data)` is `sum(0.5 * (prediction - target)^2) / T`; it retains the original factor, fixture, 0.08 training learning rate, 0.02 balancing coefficient, 1.25 capacity factor, 1,500 epochs, validation errors, update ordering, and tolerances. The lesson explicitly distinguishes this half-MSE from Chapter 1's ordinary MSE.
- Capacity is `max(1, ceil((T/E) * capacity_factor))`. Attempted hard counts are measured before admission and divided by all T to form `f_e`; full-softmax probabilities over the same T form `P_e`.
- Chapter 48 owns `L_balance = alpha * E * sum_e(f_e * P_e)`. Hard frequencies are stop-gradient and only `P_e` is differentiated. Capacity admission and dropped counts stay separate.
- The capacity-aware training objective has zero task contribution for dropped examples, but accepted task-gradient sums still divide by all attempted T. The new two-example regression test admits one, drops one, and checks that exact denominator.
- Evaluation remains uncapped: `Model::loss` routes and predicts every example, excludes the balancing term, and does not simulate capacity. Route counts and final-epoch drops remain separate output metrics.
