## Loss, shape, and evaluation conventions

- A sequence of `time + 1` scalar values yields `time` inputs, predictions, and targets.
- RNN forward stores `time + 1` states including the initial zero state. LSTM forward stores one `LstmStep` cache entry per input.
- Both model losses are ordinary MSE: the sum of squared prediction errors divided by `values.len() - 1`. There is no half factor. The analytic gradients use the same denominator.
- One epoch is one full-sequence gradient update. Validation remains chronological, explicitly teacher-forced, and separate from the persistence MSE baseline.
