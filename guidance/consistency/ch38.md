## Loss, shapes, and training units

`Decoder::loss_and_gradient` receives input IDs `[T]` and explicit shifted targets `[T]`. Its loss and gradient are means over T targets. The public accumulation method gives all M microbatches the same T, evaluates them at unchanged parameters, averages their mean gradients and data losses equally, clips the combined gradient once, and performs one AdamW optimizer update. An unequal-size extension must use `sum(T_i * g_i) / sum(T_i)` and apply the same weighting to data loss; the current public method does not accept unequal T values.

The returned loss is the mean data cross-entropy. Decoupled weight decay changes the AdamW parameter update but is absent from the returned loss and moment gradient. `step` and `total_steps` count optimizer updates. M is microbatches per update, T is target tokens per microbatch, and M times T is the effective target count for one update. The project has no epoch counter; its deterministic cursor cycles fixed-length token windows.
