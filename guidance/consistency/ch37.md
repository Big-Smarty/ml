## Limits and unresolved issues

- Whole-document O(N²) deduplication is a small-corpus teaching oracle, not a scalable index.
- FNV-1a is a stable teaching identity, not cryptographic authentication.
- The detector does not cover substring reuse, embedded benchmark items, or semantic paraphrases.
- The fixture does not establish language, personal-data, rights, representativeness, or model-quality claims.
- A split artifact records a boundary but cannot make downstream token-window code honor it.
- No Chapter 37 issue remains unresolved.
