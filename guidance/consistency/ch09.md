## Shapes and reduction

- Matrices are contiguous row-major. Inputs are `[batch,in_features]`, weights are `[out_features,in_features]`, bias is `[out_features]`, and outputs are `[batch,out_features]`.
- `backward(inputs, output_gradients)` returns gradients with those corresponding input, weight, and bias shapes.
- Chapter 9 implements no loss. The upstream scalar loss owns its sum or mean reduction and encodes it in `output_gradients`; the dense backward pass adds no second batch average.
