## Prerequisite and limitation audit

Each chapter retrieves the needed earlier knowledge at the point of use: classification/embedding→byte loss, bytes→BPE, dot products/softmax/reverse mode→attention, attention/residuals/variance→decoder, leakage/sets→corpus, weighted means/moments→trainer, then the whole path→capstone. GPU hardware is not a prerequisite for this section's CPU numerical milestones. All symbols, axes, weight orientations and loss reductions are explained beside actual worked values before requiring implementation.

No substantive topic was silently replaced by a browser illustration or made optional. The decoder is complete and actually trains; learners implement substantial forward/gradient/optimizer/integration seams while reference storage and full remaining backward are supplied. BPE is complete but remains its own explicit comparison as approved. Corpus parsing/provenance/dedup/splits are real, but do not certify a corpus as lawful, representative or contamination-free. Empty evaluation splits are reported, never called a pass. Default tiny training cannot establish useful general language quality. Explicit large and GPU reference commands remain available but were not rerun as a condition of the bounded CPU redesign.

The corpus goal check was also strengthened after integration review: it now compares ordered retained records and each full removed record, survivor ID and similarity (atol1e−6) against the independent reference, not just counts. A deliberately wrong-survivor callback that preserves counts and points to a valid retained ID is rejected by a regression test.
