The workshop clock ticks beside the window. A student writes a small program, reads its output, and changes one idea. The program learns that a question should lead to an answer. Another student checks the arithmetic and records the result. Small examples reveal mistakes before large runs make them expensive.

At dusk the group closes their laptops. They can explain each prediction as a chain of bytes, embeddings, attention, and learned weights. Tomorrow they will test the model on words it did not see during training. This course-authored text is released under CC0 for the bundled teaching demonstration.
